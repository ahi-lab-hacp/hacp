# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

Participants must be constructive, assume good faith while evaluating evidence, respect privacy, avoid harassment and discrimination, and keep disagreement focused on ideas and implementation consequences. Security concerns must be handled through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sustained disruption, deliberate disclosure of private information, or using project spaces to target individuals or groups.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure.

Report conduct concerns to **hello@ahi-lab.com**. Reports will be reviewed confidentially by project maintainers who are not directly involved in the incident whenever possible.
